🍪 Zemivo Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Zemivo may use cookies and similar technologies when you use our platform.

Important: This page describes the planned use of cookies and similar technologies. Actual cookie usage may change as Zemivo features and services are developed.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website may store in your browser or device.

They can help a website remember information about your session, preferences and interactions.

2. Why Zemivo May Use Cookies

Zemivo may use cookies and similar technologies for several purposes, including:

  • Keeping users signed in.
  • Maintaining secure sessions.
  • Remembering user preferences.
  • Remembering language or interface settings.
  • Improving website performance.
  • Understanding how features are used.
  • Detecting suspicious activity.

3. Essential Cookies

Some cookies may be necessary for basic functions of Zemivo to work correctly.

  • Login and authentication sessions.
  • Security functions.
  • Account protection.
  • Basic website functionality.
Essential technologies may be required for certain Zemivo features to function properly.

4. Preference Cookies

Preference technologies may help Zemivo remember choices made by users.

  • Language preferences.
  • Interface preferences.
  • Other selected settings.

5. Analytics

Zemivo may use analytics technologies in the future to understand how people use the platform.

Analytics may help us understand which features are useful, identify technical problems and improve the user experience.

6. Security Technologies

Zemivo may use cookies or similar technologies to help protect accounts and detect suspicious activity.

  • Detect unusual login activity.
  • Help prevent automated abuse.
  • Protect sessions.
  • Support account security.

7. Advertising Technologies

If advertising services are introduced to Zemivo, third-party advertising technologies may be used where legally permitted.

Advertising technologies may be used to measure advertising performance or provide relevant advertising.

Advertising and tracking features should only be enabled after the required technical, privacy and legal requirements have been properly implemented.

8. Third-Party Services

Some Zemivo features may depend on third-party services. Those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Examples may include authentication, analytics, payment, advertising or other technology providers.

9. Managing Cookies

Depending on your browser and device, you may be able to control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some Zemivo features.

10. Cookie Choices

If Zemivo introduces optional cookies or tracking technologies that require user consent, appropriate controls may be provided where required.

11. Changes to This Policy

Zemivo may update this Cookie Policy as the platform, technologies and legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact Zemivo

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, you can contact Zemivo Support.
