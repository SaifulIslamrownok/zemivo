🤝 Zemivo Community Guidelines

Zemivo is a community where people can connect, communicate, create content, discover ideas and build meaningful communities.

Our goal: Keep Zemivo welcoming, respectful, safe and useful for everyone.

These guidelines apply to posts, comments, photos, videos, messages, groups, pages, profiles and other community features.

1. Be Respectful

Treat other people with respect even when you disagree with them.

  • Do not intentionally insult or humiliate people.
  • Do not repeatedly target another user.
  • Do not threaten other users.
  • Do not encourage harassment.
  • Respect different opinions and backgrounds.

2. No Harassment or Bullying

Zemivo does not allow targeted harassment or abusive behavior intended to intimidate or harm another person.

  • Repeated unwanted contact
  • Threatening messages
  • Personal attacks
  • Coordinated harassment
  • Sharing someone's private information to harm them

3. No Fraud or Scams

Users must not use Zemivo to deceive people or steal money, accounts or personal information.

  • Fake investment schemes
  • Phishing links
  • Fake giveaways
  • Fraudulent marketplace listings
  • Account-stealing attempts
  • False payment requests
Warning: Fraudulent activity may result in account restrictions, suspension or termination.

4. Spam and Fake Engagement

Do not artificially manipulate engagement or overwhelm the community with unwanted content.

  • Mass unsolicited messages
  • Repeated unwanted posts
  • Fake likes or followers
  • Automated engagement manipulation
  • Artificially inflating views or comments

5. Authentic Identity

Do not impersonate another person, creator, business, organization or Zemivo representative.

  • Do not pretend to be another user.
  • Do not create deceptive official-looking accounts.
  • Do not misrepresent your relationship with Zemivo.

6. Dangerous or Illegal Activity

Zemivo must not be used to facilitate illegal or dangerous activities.

  • Illegal transactions
  • Cyber abuse
  • Malware distribution
  • Account theft
  • Threats of violence
  • Other unlawful activities

7. Sexual or Inappropriate Content

Users should not upload content that violates applicable laws or Zemivo's safety standards.

  • Sexual exploitation
  • Sexual content involving minors
  • Non-consensual intimate content
  • Content intended to sexually exploit another person
Content involving the sexual exploitation of minors is strictly prohibited and may be reported to the appropriate authorities where required.

8. Privacy

Respect the privacy of other people.

  • Do not publish private personal information without permission.
  • Do not expose private contact information to harm someone.
  • Do not share private conversations maliciously.
  • Do not attempt to access another person's account.

9. Copyright and Intellectual Property

Only upload or share content that you have the right to use.

  • Respect copyright.
  • Respect trademarks.
  • Do not claim another person's work as your own.
  • Do not distribute copyrighted material without authorization.

10. Marketplace Safety

Sellers and buyers should use Zemivo marketplace features responsibly.

  • Use accurate product descriptions.
  • Do not create fraudulent listings.
  • Do not intentionally deceive buyers.
  • Do not use the marketplace for prohibited transactions.

11. Creator and Affiliate Content

Creators and affiliates must provide honest information about products, services and promotions.

  • Do not promote fraudulent products.
  • Do not manipulate engagement to obtain rewards.
  • Clearly identify promotional content when required.
  • Do not make intentionally deceptive claims.

12. Games and Rewards

Game and reward features must not be manipulated.

  • Do not use bots to obtain rewards.
  • Do not exploit bugs for unfair rewards.
  • Do not create fake accounts to abuse reward systems.
  • Do not attempt to manipulate game results.

13. Protect Your Account

Users should take reasonable steps to protect their accounts.

  • Use a strong password.
  • Do not share your password.
  • Use two-factor authentication when available.
  • Review suspicious login activity.

14. How to Report Content

If you see content that violates these guidelines, report it through the available reporting tools.

🚨 Report Content

15. What Happens After a Report?

Reports may be reviewed using Zemivo's moderation processes.

Depending on the circumstances, Zemivo may remove content, limit features, issue warnings, suspend accounts or take other appropriate action.

Not every reported item will necessarily be removed. Decisions may depend on the content, context, applicable rules and available evidence.

16. Keep Zemivo Helpful

The best Zemivo community is one where people can create, communicate, learn, discover and earn responsibly.

Think before you post.
Be respectful. Be honest. Protect yourself and others.
